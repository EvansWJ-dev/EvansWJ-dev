## 📚 Published Writing

### MEDDLESOME TIME

*Time doesn’t heal cleanly—it revises what you remember, and it keeps revising.*

**MEDDLESOME TIME** is a literary collection of three novellas and two poems, accompanied by curated playlists, brief notes, and a screenplay adaptation of *The Ebbing Light*.

The collection explores memory, grief, coming-of-age, friendship, family, authority, and the lasting consequences of our choices.

**Included works:**

- **The Solemn Curfew** — A seventeen-year-old confronts family expectations, restrictions, and the complicated boundaries between love and control.
- **The Ebbing Light** — Following the death of a loved one, Nora Solomon navigates displacement, strained relationships, and a reckoning with her past.
- **The Rose-Thorned Hills** — John Laska struggles to make sense of a fractured life as memories arrive in fragments, time loops back, and the past refuses to stay put.
